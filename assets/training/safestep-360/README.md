# SafeStep 360 Hazard Training Prototype

**Project:** SafeStep  
**Team / Company:** Laliguras  
**Module:** CET257 Enterprise Project  
**Assessment:** A7 – Presentation of Initial Solution Ideas / Prototypes to Client

---

## Overview

This prototype demonstrates the proposed SafeStep interactive workplace health and safety training experience.

The prototype is designed for the A7 client presentation and allows a learner to:

1. Enter the SafeStep training environment.
2. Select an available training module.
3. Review the module brief and learning outcome.
4. Explore an interactive hazard-identification scenario.
5. Identify hazards and receive immediate corrective-action feedback.
6. Complete a short knowledge assessment.
7. Review the final training result.

The interface also includes Laliguras branding using the official logo provided by the team.

---

## Included Training Modules

### 1. Warehouse Hazard Training — Complete

This module demonstrates warehouse and manual-handling hazard awareness.

The learner must identify four hazards:

- Liquid spill on a walking route.
- Unsafe manual-handling posture.
- Obstructed emergency exit.
- Unsafe storage / stacked boxes.

After each hazard is selected, SafeStep explains:

- what the hazard is;
- why it creates a risk; and
- the safer corrective action.

The learner then completes a three-question knowledge check.

---

### 2. Working at Height — Complete

This module demonstrates common work-at-height hazards and fall-prevention controls.

The learner must identify four hazards:

- Unsafe or unsecured ladder setup.
- Worker close to an unprotected roof edge.
- Unsecured tools or materials creating a falling-object risk.
- Incomplete scaffold edge protection.

After each hazard is selected, the prototype gives immediate feedback explaining the safer control.

The learner then completes a three-question knowledge check.

---

### 3. Fire Safety — Coming Soon

Fire Safety is intentionally shown as a **Coming Soon** module.

The prototype does not allow the module to be launched.

This represents a future SafeStep training module covering areas such as:

- fire prevention;
- alarm response;
- evacuation;
- extinguisher awareness; and
- emergency procedures.

## How to Run the Prototype

### Requirements

No installation or web server is required.

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox

### Steps

1. Extract the prototype ZIP file.
2. Keep these files in the same folder:

   - `SafeStep_360_Hazard_Prototype_COMPLETE_WITH_LOGO.html`
   - `laliguras_logo.png`
   - `README.md`

3. Open:

   `SafeStep_360_Hazard_Prototype_COMPLETE_WITH_LOGO.html`

4. The prototype should open directly in the browser.

## Recommended A7 Client Demonstration Flow

### Step 1 — Welcome Screen

Explain that SafeStep combines practical hazard identification with feedback and assessment.

Select:

**Enter Training**

---

### Step 2 — Training Dashboard

Show the three modules:

- Warehouse Hazard Training — Available
- Working at Height — Available
- Fire Safety — Coming Soon

Explain that the prototype demonstrates two complete learning journeys while Fire Safety represents future content.

---

### Step 3 — Warehouse Hazard Training

Open the Warehouse module.

Briefly explain:

- module purpose;
- learning outcome;
- four-hazard requirement;
- interactive scenario; and
- knowledge check.

**Begin 360° Scenario**

### Step 4 — Warehouse Knowledge Check

Complete all three questions.

Show:

- question progress;
- scoring; and
- final module result.

Return to the dashboard and open **Working at Height**.

Explain the four scenario hazards:

- ladder safety;
- roof-edge protection;

- scaffold protection.

Identify each hazard and review the recommended control.

---

### Step 6 — Working at Height Knowledge Check

Complete the three-question assessment and show the final result.

---

Return to the dashboard.

Show that Fire Safety is clearly marked:

**Coming Soon**

## Scoring

Each identified hazard is worth **10 points**.

Each correct knowledge-check answer is worth **10 points**.

Maximum score per completed module:

**70 points**

- 4 hazards = 40 points
- 3 assessment questions = 30 points

The prototype uses the score to determine whether the learner receives a successful completion or a review recommendation.

## Prototype Scope and Limitations

This is an **academic demonstration prototype** rather than a production learning-management system.

The following are intentionally outside the current prototype:

- real user authentication;
- database persistence;
- organisation-level administration;
- multi-tenant client portals;
- automated reporting;
- production certificates;
- real accreditation;
- live analytics;
- server-side form processing;
- production 360° panoramic media;
- formal regulatory compliance functionality.

These areas may form part of future SafeStep development.

## Important Safety Note

The safety content in this prototype is illustrative and is intended to demonstrate the proposed interaction and learning flow.

It must **not** be represented as:

- accredited training;
- professional safety certification;
- site-specific risk assessment; or
- formal legal or regulatory advice.

Production safety content should receive appropriate subject-matter review before being presented as compliance-ready.
