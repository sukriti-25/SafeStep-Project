About this project

SafeStep is a student workplace-safety training project by Laliguras. The demo-request form sends enquiries to the project contact, Ishan, at ishanbhan61@gmail.com.

The training platform shown on this website remains a prototype. Sending an enquiry does not automatically book a demonstration.

Information you enter

The form asks for your full name, organisation name, work email, message, and consent. You can optionally provide a job title, organisation size, and service interest.

Please include only the information needed to discuss your training enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment information, medical details, or other sensitive information.

How information is used

Your details are sent over HTTPS to FormSubmit, our form-to-email service, which processes the submission and forwards it to the project contact’s Gmail inbox. We use the details to respond to your request and discuss the training needs you describe.

The email includes the submitted form fields and the form’s webpage address. Your email address lets the project contact reply directly to you. We do not add you to a marketing mailing list through this form.

Prototype reviews

The homepage review form is separate from demo requests. It accepts a rating, review text, and an optional name. Reviews appear only in the current browser tab, are labelled as local demo reviews, and disappear on reload. These review entries are not transmitted or saved.

Storage and service providers

The learner and administrator portals are local prototype features. Demo account details, profile changes, activity marks, completion dates, and certificate status are stored in this browser using local storage. They are not sent to a SafeStep server. Clearing browser data removes this prototype progress.

The website does not save demo-request entries in browser storage. Once accepted by the delivery service, submissions are processed by FormSubmit and sent to Gmail. Copies in the recipient’s inbox remain until deleted by the project contact.

FormSubmit’s documentation states that it retains submissions for 30 days, including requests awaiting form activation. See the FormSubmit documentation and FormSubmit privacy policy for its handling of data. Email is also processed under Google’s privacy policy.

Form delivery providers and the website host may process connection information for delivery, security, and spam prevention. Your browser may offer autofill according to its own settings. The website has no advertising or analytics scripts.

If submission fails or cannot be confirmed, your entries remain in the form so you can decide whether to retry or contact us directly.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact ishanbhan61@gmail.com with questions about your enquiry or to request removal of messages held by the project contact.
