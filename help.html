Help & support

Answers for training, results, certificates and account access.

How do I start a training activity?

Open My Learning from the dashboard and select Start activity. Read the module brief, begin the scenario, find all four hazards, then complete the three-question assessment.

How are my marks calculated?

Each activity awards up to 70 raw points: 40 for finding four hazards and 30 for three assessment answers. SafeStep converts the result into a percentage, so 70 points appears as 100%.

How do I earn my certificate?

Complete both Warehouse Hazard Training and Working at Height. After both are finished, your certificate unlocks automatically in the learner dashboard.

Can I repeat an activity?

Yes. Select Repeat activity from the result screen or learner dashboard. Your newest result replaces the previous result for that activity.

How do I save or download my certificate?

Open the certificate page and choose Print / Save PDF. In the browser print window, select Save as PDF to download a copy.

Where is my progress stored?

This student prototype stores account and progress information only in this browser. Clearing browser storage resets the prototype data.
