About SafeStep

Better learning.

Behind every safety procedure is a person. We’re building a more practical way to help people understand risks and make informed choices at work.

Rooted in people.

Why we started

Bridge the gap between
learning and doing.

Workplace training can become an exercise in reading and remembering. SafeStep explores how digital learning can make that experience more relevant, active, and accessible.

As a student project, we bring together research, design, and development to prototype a workplace health-and-safety learning platform. Our focus is simple: give people useful opportunities to practise before they face a decision at work.

Our mission

SafeStep makes workplace safety training practical, engaging, and accessible through interactive digital learning.

Our vision

A working world where every person can access meaningful safety learning and feel better prepared for the risks around them.

What guides us

Purpose in every decision.

Our values shape the content we create and the experiences we design.

Safety first

Put people’s wellbeing at the centre and frame learning around everyday workplace risks.

Accessible learning

Design clear content, flexible pacing, and usable interfaces for a range of learning needs.

Practical innovation

Use technology where it helps people understand, practise, or reflect more effectively.

Client collaboration

Listen to workplace needs and use feedback to shape relevant learning experiences.
